The Sugar Bites Story

Founded in 2024 with a simple mission: to create the most delicious, high-quality baked goods using traditional recipes and modern techniques.

What started as a small home bakery has grown into a beloved local destination for cookie lovers, latte enthusiasts, and waffle connoisseurs. Every item we make is crafted with love, using only the finest ingredients sourced from local farmers and ethical suppliers.

Our name "Sugar Bites" represents the little moments of joy we bring to our customers' lives - one sweet bite at a time.
